A few helpful tips

Keep everything clear and useful.

Provide enough detail for someone to understand the information without needing to ask you to explain it again.

  • Use a clear title for your submission.
  • Select the category that fits best.
  • Include relevant details in the description.
  • Provide a valid email for follow-up.
Your submission will be sent to the website's configured backend. Avoid including passwords, financial credentials or other sensitive personal information.
